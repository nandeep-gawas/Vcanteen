Design Rules

Goal

Make the website feel like a real student-built product designed by a human designer, not an AI-generated landing page.

Visual direction

- Minimal
- Editorial
- Functional
- High contrast
- Spacious
- Calm
- Professional
- Slightly technical

Avoid

- Purple/blue/pink gradient backgrounds
- Neon glow effects
- Decorative blurred circles
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Excessive rounded cards
- Gradient text
- Gradient borders
- Excessive drop shadows
- Random icons used as decoration
- Floating cards everywhere
- Excessive hover scaling
- Confetti
- Generic AI marketing language

Colors

Prefer:

- near-black
- warm white
- neutral gray
- one restrained accent color

Use color to communicate meaning, not decoration.

Typography

Use strong typography and spacing rather than gradients or effects to create visual hierarchy.

Headings should be confident and simple.

Body text should be short, concrete and readable.

Layout

Prefer:

- asymmetric layouts
- editorial sections
- lists
- tables
- thin dividers
- generous whitespace
- simple grids when appropriate

Avoid generic:

- three identical feature cards
- icon + heading + paragraph repeated three times
- floating glass cards
- centered content everywhere

Components

Components should have a clear functional reason to exist.

Do not add an icon simply because there is empty space.

Use Lucide icons sparingly.

Motion

Motion should communicate state or interaction.

Prefer subtle transitions around 150–250ms.

Do not use animation simply to make the page look impressive.

Copywriting

Write like a real hackathon organizer.

Avoid:

- Transform
- Empower
- Supercharge
- Next-gen
- Seamless
- Revolutionary
- Unlock your potential
- Take your ideas to the next level
- AI-powered future

Prefer concrete information.

Bad:
"Transform your ideas into revolutionary solutions."

Good:
"Build a working prototype in 24 hours."

Important

Before adding a new visual effect, ask whether the effect improves usability.

When uncertain, choose the simpler design.
